# Credits

Subsidence is mostly made from scratch: the floors, the entities, the items and
much of the sound are built for the game. What comes from elsewhere is listed
here. All of it is public domain (CC0), released under a zero-attribution
licence, or openly licensed (fonts under the SIL Open Font License). Attribution
is kept even where it is not required, so every source stays traceable.

### Made for the game

Everything else is original and modelled for Subsidence in Blender from
scratch: every entity (the Rictus, Seamstress, Lurcher, Uninvited, Strider,
Goblin, Drowner, Watcher, Scuttler and Mannequin), every item (the torch, field
receiver, kitchen knife, pistol, pipe, pipe wrench, extinguisher, screwdriver,
fuse, battery and the Solace bottle), and the furniture and fittings of every
floor, from the office desks and water cooler to the parked car, hotel room 610,
the metro's maintenance room and the last floor's fuse stand.

## Recorded audio

Every recording is **CC0 / public domain**. Each was trimmed, mixed to mono,
cleaned and re-encoded for the web; distance, direction and reverb are applied
by the game as it plays them.

The voices of the Strider, Drowner, Mannequin and Glimmer, and the shell sounds
of the Scuttler, are original procedural sound design with no recordings in
them (the Scuttler's steps add rubberduck's CC0 splashes, credited under
Footsteps and water). The music and most ambience are synthesized in code.
